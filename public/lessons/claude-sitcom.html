Claude Sitcom

Mockumentary comedies written and animated by Claude, one for each Year 8 English topic. About ten minutes each, with a documentary crew, a Year 8 class in Singapore and a cast of very opinionated words, punctuation marks and literary ghosts. Every key idea, quotation and exam trap is in there.
